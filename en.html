This policy explains how information is handled in the iOS app “TB PartyGame” (the “App”), published by Katsumi Sakurai (“we”).

1. Information we do not collect

The App requires no account and we operate no server. Player names you enter and game results stay on your device and are never transmitted. We do not collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or photos.

2. Advertising

The App displays advertising through Google AdMob. To deliver, measure, and protect those ads, AdMob may automatically collect:

CategoryDetails
Advertising identifiersIDFA and identifiers issued by AdMob
Device informationDevice model, OS version, language, screen size
Coarse locationCountry or region inferred from IP address
Ad interactionsImpressions and taps

This data is collected and used by Google; we do not receive it directly. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. App Tracking Transparency

On first launch the App may present Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. Only if you choose “Allow” is your advertising identifier used to personalize ads. If you decline, you will see non-personalized ads and every feature of the App remains fully available.

You can change this at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App presents a consent form via Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can revisit and change your consent choices from within the App at any time.

5. In-app purchase

The App offers a “Remove Ads” in-app purchase. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment details. Purchase status is determined on your device from information provided by Apple.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as laws change or as the App gains features. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
